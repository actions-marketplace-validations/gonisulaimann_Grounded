# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please **do not open a public issue**. Use GitHub's private vulnerability
reporting: the **Security** tab of this repository, then **Report a
vulnerability**. Include:

- the `grounded --version` output,
- a minimal fixture reproducing the problem,
- what you expected vs what happened.

We aim to acknowledge within 7 days and will keep you updated on a fix and
release timeline.
